Privacy Policy

Diff Race · Last updated October 16, 2026

Diff Race is a small free multiplayer car-combat racing game, playable on its website (diff-race.netlify.app) and as a Discord Activity. This policy explains what data the game uses. In short: what's needed to race together while you're in a room, plus a public leaderboard of room-race results; never sold, and never used for ads.

What we collect and why

DataWhyHow long
Your driver name: typed on the website, or your Discord display name in the Discord Activity Shown to the other drivers in your room (standings, name tags, the kill feed) and on the leaderboard In a room: until you leave it. On the leaderboard: see below
Race data: your car's position, speed, turret aim, armour, damage dealt, kills, lap and finish times, and in-race events such as wrecks, missiles and dropped hazards Needed to sync the race between drivers Until you leave the room; events are deleted after about 3 seconds, hazards when they run out
Room details: the room's name and code, its track settings, the names of who's in it, and its last race's results To list open rooms, let others join, and show how the last race finished Deleted when the last driver leaves the room
Leaderboard entry: an identifier (a random one made by your browser, or your Discord user ID in the Activity), your driver name, and your races, podiums, wins, kills and score from room races The public global leaderboard Kept until you ask us to remove it
Discord account basics (Discord Activity only): your Discord user ID, username and display name, via the identify permission; and the rpc.activities.write permission To sign you in and show your name; your user ID is your leaderboard identifier; the second permission shows your race (for example "Lap 2/3 · 3rd") as your Discord activity status Only your display name and user ID are stored (see above); the access token isn't kept
Settings on your device: your driver name, controls, video and sound settings, best lap times per track, and your browser's leaderboard identifier So the game remembers you and your preferences In your browser's local storage until you clear it

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no cookies.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone beyond the services below that run the game.
  • We don't access your Discord messages, servers, friends list or email address.

Services that process data for us

  • Google Firebase Realtime Database (Singapore region) stores the room, race and leaderboard data described above. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Netlify hosts the website and the small server function used for Discord sign-in. Like any web host, it processes technical request data such as IP addresses. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Discord, when you play the Activity. Discord's own Privacy Policy applies to your use of Discord.

Who can see your data

Everything in a room (your name and race data) is visible to the other drivers in it, the list of open rooms is public, and so is the leaderboard. Please don't use personal information such as your real full name as your driver name.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Discord requires its users to be at least 13 (or older where local law requires).

Your choices

You can leave a room at any time, which removes your data from it. You can clear the game's saved settings by clearing your browser's site data. To ask about your data, or to have your leaderboard entry removed, message thejenos on Discord.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: message thejenos on Discord.
