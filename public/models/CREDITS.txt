Models from Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
Car Kit 3.1, City Kit Industrial 2.0 and Racing Kit 2.0, rebuilt for Diff Race by scripts/build-cars.mjs
and scripts/build-props.mjs.

Surface textures from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
asphalt_track, sandy_gravel, dirt_aerial_02, concrete_wall_008 and metal_plate, resized and recompressed
for Diff Race by scripts/build-textures.mjs.

Realistic props from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0: Barrel_01, Barrel_02, old_tyre, rock_07 and
rock_09, simplified and their textures shrunk for Diff Race by scripts/build-real.mjs.

The realistic car: "Car Concept" by Eric Chadwick, Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH (2024), from the Khronos
glTF Sample Assets (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept), licensed
CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Based on "Free Concept Car 004" by Unity Fan (CC0).
Changed for Diff Race by scripts/build-real.mjs: the interior, wipers, number plate and brake callipers removed,
the Khronos and 3D Commerce logos removed from the tyres, rims and mirrors, the meshes simplified, the materials
made plain (no clear coat, transmission or variants) and the textures shrunk. The game repaints the body and
